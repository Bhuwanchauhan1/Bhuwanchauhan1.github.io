Amadeus Strategy In progress

A clearer view
of the market.

Building a repeatable market and competitive intelligence framework to contribute to the 2027 planning cycle.

Read the project: A clearer view of the market

The context

Planning benefits from a consistent view of the market. As part of the Strategy and Planning team at Amadeus, I’m contributing to the 2027 planning cycle.

My contribution

I’m building a repeatable framework for market and competitive intelligence, helping organize inputs so they can be compared and revisited during planning.

What I’m working through

  • How to structure market and competitor information consistently.
  • How to make the framework repeatable rather than a one-off research exercise.
  • How to keep the output useful for planning discussions.

Ongoing planning contribution. Internal research and planning documents are not shared publicly.

Amadeus Finance & AI Pilot

Making reconciliation
less manual.

An AI-assisted agent comparing Workfront actuals with finance reports, being piloted with regional teams.

Read the project: Making reconciliation less manual

The context

Finance reconciliation requires comparing information from different sources. This work focuses on Workfront actuals and finance reports.

My contribution

I’m building an AI-assisted reconciliation agent to support that comparison and piloting it with teams in EMEA, APAC and the Americas.

What makes it interesting

It brings finance and practical AI into the same workflow. The useful question is how assistance can make a recurring comparison clearer and easier to review.

Regional pilot in progress. Source financial records remain private.

Read the project: Less searching, more finding

The context

A shared workspace is useful when people can understand its structure. This work spans SharePoint and Teams within Amadeus.

My contribution

I’m supporting a more organized digital workspace and drafting workspace guidelines. The focus is on a clearer structure and practical guidance for using it.

What comes first

The foundation matters: organize the workspace before adding more tools. A search agent is a possible future idea, not a delivered part of this project.

Amadeus Customer insight In progress

Finding themes
in feedback.

Developing topic and keyword rules in Medallia Text Analytics to organize customer survey feedback.

Read the project: Finding themes in feedback

The context

Open-text survey responses contain useful detail. Grouping recurring themes can make that feedback easier to work with.

My contribution

I’m developing topic and keyword rules in Medallia Text Analytics for customer survey feedback at Amadeus.

The learning

This is hands-on work with the language customers use and the rules that organize it. It is an opportunity to learn how a useful topic structure develops through careful iteration.

Topic and keyword rule development is ongoing.

Acadawn Group Finance 2024–25

Getting closer
to the numbers.

Profitability and market analysis for digital product launches, supported by dashboards for revenue, margins and conversion.

Read the project: Getting closer to the numbers

The context

At Acadawn Group, I worked remotely as a Business and Financial Analyst from August 2024 to March 2025.

My contribution

I carried out profitability and market analysis for digital product launches, worked on resource allocation modelling, and built dashboards to track revenue, margins and conversion.

The outcome

The analysis supported work associated with 30–40% revenue growth and approximately 20% better cost efficiency at Acadawn. These were supported business outcomes, not growth caused by my work alone.

Outcomes supported at Acadawn Group, not Amadeus. The illustration is not a financial chart.

Paris bookshop Operations 2025

operations.

Improving operational and financial tracking at Librairie du Cinéma du Panthéon, an independent Paris bookshop.

Read the project: Better everyday operations

The context

From May to December 2025, I worked as an Operations and Accounts Assistant at Librairie du Cinéma du Panthéon in Paris.

My contribution

I managed day-to-day operations and financial tracking and redesigned the tracking process to improve data accuracy. I also supported the bookshop’s digital content effort.

The outcome

The tracking redesign improved data accuracy by approximately 20%. The digital content effort I supported reached over 500,000 organic views.

Outcomes relate to the Paris bookshop and the work I supported there.
