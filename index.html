I build the tools, not just the slides. At Amadeus I designed and shipped an AI agent that automates planning and reporting, saving hours of manual data collection every month. I use generative AI and workflow automation to strip out the busywork, so the team spends its time on judgement and decisions instead of copying numbers between spreadsheets.
